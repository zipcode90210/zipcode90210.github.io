zip code 90210 is a collaborative project by Michaela Moravčíková and Timotej Kosmel
@michaelamoravcikova & @timo_kosmeel

both members of the Æ painting group since 2022
@_ae_painters

Michaela (b. 1991, Slovakia) lives and works in Hamburg. Her practice moves between painting, printmaking, drawing, and writing. Her non-figurative work is characterized by gestural and physical expressions that merge lyrical abstraction with the spontaneity of arbitrary signs. Driven by an interest in the transformative capacities of materials and forms, her work embraces process and the unfolding of the image, drawing inspiration from fairy tales, everyday life, games, circuses, and the quiet presence of ordinary objects.

Timotej (b. 1998, Slovakia; U.S.A./Slovakia) lives and works in Vienna. His practice explores the lines of permeability between digital and analog media, ranging from drawing and printmaking through painting, with a particular emphasis on creating collaborative events (sessions). The themes of his work engage with processuality, environmental potentials, the eventfulness of social space as a spectrum of reflection, and the body as an instrument mediating the inner and outer layers of everyday life.
